What we do

Storm-proof roofing, start to finish

From a single lifted shingle to a full metal re-roof, one licensed crew handles the inspection, the permits and the build.

  • Storm Damage Repair

    Lifted shingles, cracked tiles and torn flashing found, photographed and fixed before the next storm rolls in.

  • Emergency Tarping

    Crews on call 24/7 to dry-in your roof with secured, weighted tarps as soon as it’s safe to climb.

  • Roof Replacement

    Complete tear-off and re-roof, permitted and inspected, with the deck re-nailed to current wind code.

  • Metal Roofing

    Standing-seam and 5V metal systems rated for high winds. They shed heat too, so your AC works less.

  • Tile & Shingle

    Concrete and clay tile or architectural shingles, installed with the fastening patterns Florida demands.

  • Leak Repair

    We trace the leak to its source, whether flashing, vents or valleys, and seal it before it turns into mold.

  • Roof Inspections

    Free storm and pre-season inspections with a photo report you can keep for your records.

  • Hurricane Upgrades

    Sealed roof decks, upgraded nailing and secondary water barriers, the upgrades that may qualify for wind-mitigation discounts.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you tarp my roof after a storm?

Usually the same day. Our storm line is answered 24/7, and a crew heads out as soon as it’s safe to be on the roof. Tarps are secured and weighted, so they hold until the permanent repair.

Are your inspections really free?

Yes. We inspect the roof, photograph every issue we find and give you a written report and quote. There’s no obligation and no pressure.

Do I need a new roof, or just a repair?

Most storm damage can be repaired. We’ll show you photos of exactly what we found and explain both options. If a repair will do the job, that’s what we’ll recommend.

What roof types do you install?

Architectural shingle, standing-seam and 5V metal, concrete and clay tile, and low-slope systems like TPO and modified bitumen, all installed to Florida Building Code.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes take one to three days on the roof, depending on size and material. We handle the permit before we start and schedule the final inspection when we’re done.

Don’t wait for the next storm. Is your roof ready?

Hurricane season runs June through November. Book a free inspection and find out before the next storm does.
